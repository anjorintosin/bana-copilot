About

          
Bana Copilot is an intelligent stealth overlay that sits on your screen during interviews. It listens when you ask it to, inspects what’s on screen when sent, and drafts concise suggestions — never auto-answering without you.

          
Desktop 0.1.6

          
What you can do

          
    
            
  • Ask anything — type in the top bar and press Enter (↵). Ask follow-ups, coding inquiries, or questions you heard.
    • 
            
  • Assist — the top-right Assist button drafts answers based on interview speech and screen context.
    • 
            
  • Move HUD — reposition the overlay anywhere on your display using ⌘↑, ⌘↓, ⌘←, and ⌘→.
    • 
            
  • Visibility / Stealth — the slashed eye icon ensures the HUD remains invisible to Zoom and Google Meet screen share. When visible, it alerts with an orange highlight.
    • 
            
  • Solve screen — tap the screen icon to capture coding problems, interview prompts, or diagrams on your monitor.
    • 
            
  • Modes & Knowledge — customize prompt context (e.g. Coding vs System Design) and attach resumes or job descriptions.
    • 
            
  • History — browse and review past session transcripts and answers anytime.
    • 
          

          
How to use it

          
    
            
  1. Check permissions for Microphone, Screen Recording, and Meeting Audio in Onboarding.
    2. 
            
  2. Start a session from the HUD when your interview or meeting begins.
    3. 
            
  3. When asked a question, press Assist (⌘Enter) or type into the wide top input bar.
    4. 
            
  4. Use ⌘↑, ⌘↓, ⌘←, and ⌘→ to move the HUD out of your way.
    5. 
            
  5. Click the back arrow (←) on the top left to collapse the HUD back to the compact pill.
